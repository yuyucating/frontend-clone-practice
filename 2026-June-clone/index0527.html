BOOK A STAY EXPLORE THE CABINS

Not a hotel.
A cleaning
in the woods.

No front desk. No restaurant. No schedule. Six cabins in a clearing in Telemark, Norway: a wood-fired sauna at the water, a cold lake past the pines, and marked trails through birch and spruce. The hours between them are yours.

FOREST TRAILS

FOURTEEN KILOMETERS - MARKED YEAR ROUND

Trails leave from behind the sauna and run north into the reserve. Three marked loops cross spruce, birch, wet stone, and a smaller lake before returning to the clearing.

THE LAKE

FOUR DEGREES - OPEN ALL YEARS

Small, deep, and spring-fed, the lake stays cold through the year. The sauna sits at the water, with a short jetty for swimming and cold plunge.
